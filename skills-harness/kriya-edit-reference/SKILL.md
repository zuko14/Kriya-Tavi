# Learning from a reference

An editor studies choices, not pixels.

1. **Only clips the person supplies**, and only after they confirm they may use them as a reference. No downloading from links.
2. **Measure, don't copy**: pace (how long shots last, and how that changes), structure (hook, first cut, ending), colour, caption style, sound levels. Numbers only.
3. **Apply to their footage**: the measures drive Kriya's own recipes and looks.
4. **Never copy** the reference's frames, wording, music, logos, faces or characters into the output.
5. **Explain the match** in plain words: "cuts every 2 to 3 seconds at the start, slower after".

**Done when** the result feels similar in pace and look, is made only of the person's own material, and the checks passed or are named.
