# Short ads

1. **One message.** Ask what the single thing is; everything serves it.
2. **The first seconds** show the product or the problem it solves.
3. **Variants.** 6, 15 and 30 seconds; vertical, square and wide. Each length tells the whole message, not a cut-off of the longest.
4. **End card** with the offer and the call to act, held long enough to read.
5. **Claims** come only from the brief and the person's material: prices, numbers and promises are never invented.
6. **Rights** for music, footage and logos are confirmed, not assumed.

**Done when** every variant carries the message and the checks passed or are named.
