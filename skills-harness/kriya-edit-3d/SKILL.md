# 3D in video

1. **Only when it adds something**: a product turned to show its sides, a screen in a device, a logo reveal. Otherwise use 2D.
2. **Simple scenes**: one object, clean lighting, a plain background that matches the video's look.
3. **Smooth moves**: slow, eased camera paths; no spins so fast they blur.
4. **Short**: a few seconds is usually enough.
5. **Rights**: models, textures and logos must be the person's own or licensed.
6. **Same frame every time**: the render is seekable and repeatable, so a re-render matches.

**Done when** the element reads at a glance and the checks passed or are named.
