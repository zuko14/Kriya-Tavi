# Numbers on screen

1. **From the file.** Every figure comes from the person's data or their own words. Never invent, estimate or "round up" a number into a claim.
2. **Say where it's from** when the source isn't obvious.
3. **Simple charts**: one point per chart, labelled axes and units, readable on a phone.
4. **Reveal, don't decorate**: animate to show the change the speaker is talking about, in time with it.
5. **Check twice**: the number on screen matches the number said and the number in the file.

**Done when** every figure on screen traces to its source and the checks passed or are named.
