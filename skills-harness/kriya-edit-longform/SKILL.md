# Long YouTube videos

1. **Story first.** Read the outline of acts and scenes before touching a cut; know what each scene is for.
2. **Scene by scene.** Cut one scene at a time and keep a one-paragraph note of where the story stands.
3. **Keep every point.** Over the length asked for, trim inside scenes so each keeps its opening and its share; don't drop a whole scene silently.
4. **Pace.** The picture should change often enough to hold attention: cuts, a punch-in, B-roll. Faster in the opening minute. Name any flat stretch.
5. **Chapters** follow the scenes: the first at 00:00, each at least ten seconds, titled by what the scene is about. Offer better titles; the person picks.
6. **Captions** go as a separate file for YouTube unless the person wants them burned in.
7. **End screen.** Keep the last 20 seconds clear of text and cards.

**Never** reorder scenes or cut a speaker's argument short without saying so.

**Done when** the cut, the chapter list and the report are ready, with notes per scene.
