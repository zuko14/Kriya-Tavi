# Screen tutorials

1. **Follow the action.** Zoom in smoothly where something happens (typing, a click, a menu), and back out before the next place.
2. **Cut the waiting**: installs, loading, long silences with nothing changing on screen.
3. **Keep the thinking** when something is happening on screen; a viewer needs it to follow.
4. **Never hide a step.** If the viewer must click it, they must see it.
5. **Readable.** Text on screen must be legible at the size people will watch; zoom rather than squint.
6. **Callouts** sparingly, only to point at what's easy to miss.

**Done when** someone could follow along from the video alone, and the checks passed or are named.
