# Colour

1. **Match first, look second.** Correct before you style.
2. **Balance** a shot only when it is visibly off against its neighbours (exposure, white balance), and only a little.
3. **Match cameras** from measured colour, one shot to another, not by eye on a thumbnail.
4. **Skin tones** stay natural; don't let a match turn faces green or orange.
5. **A look** goes last, at a strength the person chooses. A LUT from a still or a reference is a look, not a copy of that image.
6. **Every grade can be undone**: describe it as a change ("warmer, a little brighter"), not a number dump.

**Done when** shots cut together without jumps and the picture checks passed or are named.
