# Reels from raw takes

1. **Lines, not seconds.** Work line by line: the best take of each, the restarts and abandoned attempts out.
2. **The hook.** The first sentence must work alone. A stronger line may move to the front only if the story still holds; say when a line moved.
3. **Tighten, don't flatten.** Fillers, stutters and long pauses go; the speaker's own rhythm stays. A breath before a point is often worth keeping.
4. **Length.** 30 to 60 seconds unless the brief says otherwise. Drop lines that repeat or wander, never the point.
5. **Picture.** The face stays whole in the 9:16 frame. Punch in on a few words that carry weight, not every sentence.
6. **Captions** a person can read at a glance: a few words a page, away from the face and the platform's buttons.

**Never** make the speaker say something they didn't say by joining halves of different sentences.

**Done when** the reel opens on a line that stands alone and the checks passed or are named.
