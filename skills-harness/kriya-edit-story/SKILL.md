# Story structure

1. **One sentence.** What is this video saying? If it can't be said in one sentence, find that first.
2. **Hook, build, payoff.** Open on the question or the promise; build with the strongest points in an order that escalates; land the payoff, then stop.
3. **Earn the place.** Each part must move the story. Ask of every section: would anyone miss it?
4. **What to cut when everything is good**: repeats, setups for things that never pay off, the second-best example of the same point.
5. **Keep meaning.** Never reorder or trim so that the speaker seems to say something they didn't.
6. **Show the outline** to the person before cutting deep.

**Done when** the outline is agreed and every part of the cut serves it.
