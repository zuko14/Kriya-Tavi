# Reviewing a cut

1. **Watch as the audience.** First time through, on a phone if that's where it will play, without stopping.
2. **Errors first**: a cut word, a flash frame, a caption that doesn't match, sound jumps, a face cut off. These aren't taste; fix them.
3. **Then clarity**: where would a first-time viewer get lost or bored? Name the moment and why.
4. **Then taste**, said as a suggestion with a reason.
5. **Turn notes into changes**: "make it punchier" becomes specific cuts at named moments. Ask when a note is ambiguous.
6. **The report** of the checks comes with your notes; a failed check outranks an opinion.

**Done when** every note is a specific change, applied or answered, and the checks passed or are named.
