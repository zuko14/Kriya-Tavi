# Music-led edits

1. **The song leads.** Choose the music first; the edit follows its sections and energy.
2. **Fit the length** by cutting the song on its downbeats, so it starts and ends properly, not fading in the middle of a phrase.
3. **Cut on beats**, bigger changes on downbeats. Not every beat: let shots breathe in quiet parts.
4. **Energy.** The best shots go where the music lifts.
5. **Rights.** Use music the person owns or has a licence for, and say which.

**Never** use a song without knowing its rights.

**Done when** the cuts sit on the music, the song ends cleanly, and the checks passed or are named.
