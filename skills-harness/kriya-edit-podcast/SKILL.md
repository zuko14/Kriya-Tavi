# Multi-camera podcasts

1. **Sync by sound.** Line cameras up from their own audio. Report each camera's offset and how sure the match is; if it's unsure, ask instead of guessing.
2. **One sound.** The programme's sound comes from one source throughout, so no cut is heard. Camera audio is only for syncing.
3. **Switching.** The speaker's camera while one person talks; the wide when people talk over each other or laugh; a short look at the listener inside a long answer. No shot shorter than about two and a half seconds, and no cut for a one-word reply.
4. **Check the start and end.** A camera that started late can't be cut to before it was rolling.
5. **Clips.** Short clips from the episode follow the shorts rules: each must stand alone.

**Never** cut to someone in a way that changes who appears to say what.

**Done when** every camera is synced, the switching follows who speaks, and the report is clean or named.
