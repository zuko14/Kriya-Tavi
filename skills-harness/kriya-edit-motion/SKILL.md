# Motion and titles

1. **Only where it helps**: a name, a number, a step, a point worth seeing. Motion that doesn't carry meaning is noise.
2. **Readable**: on screen long enough to read twice, sized for a phone, high contrast.
3. **Out of the way**: never over a face, the speaker's point, or the platform's buttons.
4. **One style** per video: the same type, colours and easing throughout.
5. **Kriya's own blocks** first; custom animation only when the person asks and it is allowed.
6. **Words on screen** are only what the speaker said or the person wrote.

**Done when** every title reads cleanly and the text checks passed or are named.
