# Captions

1. **Exact words.** Captions show what was said. A wrong word is fixed in the transcript, never invented.
2. **Short pages**: two to five words for reels, a line or two for long videos; never split a name or a number.
3. **Readable**: high contrast, a size that reads on a phone, on screen long enough to read.
4. **Placed** away from the speaker's face and the platform's buttons.
5. **Emphasis** on a few words that carry the point, not every other word.
6. **Languages.** Keep the speaker's language and script; mixed Hindi-English or Telugu-English stays as spoken.
7. **Sidecar.** For YouTube and long videos, a separate SRT is often better than burned-in text.

**Done when** every caption matches the audio and the text checks passed or are named.
