# Shorts from long videos

1. **Stands alone.** A short is a whole micro-story: it opens without "and", "so" or "this", and ends on a finished thought.
2. **Candidates, not one guess.** Find a few, compare them on the same measures (opening, focus on one topic, pace, length, ending), and show the ranking with its reasons.
3. **Ranking isn't a forecast.** Say the order is for this video; never promise reach, views or virality.
4. **Length.** 15 to 60 seconds; tighter is usually better.
5. **Reframe.** The speaker's whole face stays in the vertical frame and the frame moves smoothly, never in jumps.
6. **Context.** If a moment needs a line from earlier to make sense, say so instead of cutting a misleading clip.

**Never** cut a clip that changes what the speaker meant.

**Done when** the person has the chosen short and knows why it beat the others.
