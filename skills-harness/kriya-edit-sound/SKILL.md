# Sound and mix

1. **Voice first.** Speech must be clear: gentle clean-up of noise and room, never so much it sounds underwater.
2. **Music under the voice**, lowered while someone speaks and back up in the gaps.
3. **Effects with restraint.** A few, each with a reason, never on top of a key word.
4. **Loudness** at the platform's target, with true peaks under the limit, measured, not guessed.
5. **Cuts** in sound must not click; a word is never cut in half.
6. **Rights** for every song and effect are known.

**Done when** the voice is clear over everything and the sound checks passed or are named.
