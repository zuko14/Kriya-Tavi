# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Kriya Tavi is built in the open by people with different backgrounds, skill levels and opinions. We want taking part to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Assume good intent; ask before you judge.
- Give and receive feedback about the work, not the person.
- Own your mistakes and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people using it.

## What we don't accept

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Trolling, deliberate derailing, or sustained disruption.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions, and any channel where someone represents the project — and to public spaces when someone is acting on its behalf.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer, **Sourav Bera**, through the contact options at <https://souravbera.vercel.app>. Reports are handled promptly and confidentially.

Maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate, including:

1. **Correction** — a private note explaining what was wrong.
2. **Warning** — a formal warning with consequences for continued behavior.
3. **Temporary ban** — no interaction with the project for a set period.
4. **Permanent ban** — for sustained or severe violations.
