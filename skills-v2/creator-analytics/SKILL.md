# Creator analytics

1. **Get the real numbers.** Use `youtube_stats` for the channel and recent videos, `instagram_insights` for the professional account and recent posts. Say which account and how many recent items you looked at.
2. **Compare like with like.** Posts of the same format, over the same span of time since publishing. Say the period. A post from yesterday can't be compared with one from last month.
3. **Simple, honest measures.** Views or reach per post, likes and comments per post, engagement as (likes + comments) ÷ followers when followers are known; show the formula once. Round sensibly and show the raw figures beside any percentage.
4. **No invented benchmarks.** Don't quote an "industry average" or a "good engagement rate" unless you found it in a source and name it. Compare the person with their own past instead.
5. **Explain, then suggest.** Name the two or three posts that did best and worst and what they share (topic, format, length, hook, posting day). Suggest at most three specific things to try, each tied to what the numbers show.
6. **Careful with cause.** Say "may" when a pattern rests on a few posts.
7. **Publishing waits.** If the person wants to post, `instagram_publish` waits for their approval of the exact image and caption; prepare it once, complete.
