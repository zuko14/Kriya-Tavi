# Security Policy

Kriya Tavi runs AI agents for many people on shared infrastructure. Keeping
each person's data separate and Kriya's credentials private comes first.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please don't open a public issue.** Email Kriya support (the address on the
site, or `KRIYA_SUPPORT_EMAIL` for your deployment) with "security" in the
subject, what you found, and how to reproduce it. We'll acknowledge it within
three working days and keep you posted until it's fixed.

Please don't access other people's data, degrade the service, or use social
engineering while testing.

## Design

The threat model, the controls for each threat and the known gaps are in
